**StitchSmart: AI-Assisted E-commerce Store**
*University of Central Punjab (UCP), Group G2*

An apparel e-commerce platform where customers can design their own clothing, get AI-powered help and shop with local payments and WhatsApp updates.

- 🎨 **Design Studio:** customers customize hoodies, crewnecks, sweatpants and shorts
- 🤖 **RAG-based AI chatbot:** context-aware customer support using Google Gemini and FastAPI
- 🔎 **Smart recommendations and live search** based on browsing and search history
- 🛍️ **Shopping features:** wishlist, product comparison, ratings and reviews, vouchers, multi-stage returns
- 💳 **JazzCash payment integration** and **WhatsApp / email order notifications**
